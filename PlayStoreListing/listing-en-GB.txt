Due SP is a city mobility app built for public transport in São Paulo.

Plan door-to-door journeys, check upcoming bus, Metro and CPTM arrivals and follow line status. One app for your daily commute.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY
• Door-to-door routes combining buses, Metro and CPTM
• Compare options by arrival time, changes and walking time
• Every leg explained: the line, where to board, stops on the way and where to get off
• Walking legs clearly marked between steps
• Route map with the colour of every line
• Save routes and search again with one tap
• The planner remembers your last journey and is easy to clear
• Share a journey as a link that opens in the app or on the web

ARRIVALS AND STATUS
• Map with stops, stations and the Metro and CPTM lines
• Search stops by name or address
• Upcoming arrivals by line and destination at every stop
• The full route of every line, with all its stops
• Service status of Metro and CPTM lines
• Disruption alerts for the lines you choose
• Favourites to keep your key stops at the top

DUE SP PREMIUM
On the free plan you can run 5 journey searches a day, always leaving now, and save 2 routes. With Premium (weekly or yearly):
• Unlimited journey searches and a choice of departure day and time
• Unlimited saved routes
• Arrivals for a custom date and time, with earlier and later arrivals

The subscription renews automatically until cancelled in Google Play.

Great for:
• getting to work and back home
• less waiting at stops and stations
• choosing the best line right now
• finding your way somewhere new in the city
